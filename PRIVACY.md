# Privacy Policy: Echoes of the Forgotten City

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026
**Developer:** oddlycodes
**Contact:** support@oddlycodes.com
**App:** Echoes of the Forgotten City (Android package `com.oddlycodes.echoesoftheforgottencity`)

## In short

- The game plays fully offline and needs no account.
- **Nothing is sent unless you agree.** On first launch the game asks whether you'd like to share
  anonymous play data. If you say no, nothing about you or your play ever leaves your device.
- If you say yes, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to
  [GameAnalytics](https://gameanalytics.com) so we can see where players get stuck and make the game
  better. It never sends your name, email, contacts, precise location or advertising ID.
- You can change your answer at any time in the in-game **Menu** (*Share play data: On/Off*).
- The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-in.

## Data that stays on your device

Your saved game and your settings (music on/off, volume, your answer about sharing play data) are
stored only on your device, in the game's private storage. We never receive them. Uninstalling the
game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

## Anonymous play data (only if you agree)

When you agree to share play data, the game sends the following to GameAnalytics over an encrypted
(HTTPS) connection:

- **Gameplay events:** when you start and finish the chapter and each objective, how many minutes of
  play each objective takes, which clues (Echoes) you find, right and wrong answers in the Chronicle
  and the valve puzzle, which lines of conversation you reach (for example, whether you ask Pell for
  a hint), and which button you choose on the end-of-demo screen.
- **Session information:** when a play session starts and ends, how long it lasts, and how many
  sessions you have played.
- **Device information:** device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, and the
  game's version.
- **Error reports:** technical details if the game hits an error or crashes.
- **A random ID** created by the game on your device, so that events from the same device can be
  grouped together. It is not your advertising ID, Android ID or any other device identifier, and it
  is not linked to your name or any account. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its
  data.

Like any internet service, GameAnalytics receives your device's IP address when data is sent. It
uses it to work out your approximate country, so we can see roughly where players are. We never
see your IP address or precise location.

### How it is used

Only to understand how people play and to improve the game: for example, which objectives take too
long, which puzzles are too hard, and which devices have problems. We do not sell it, use it for
advertising, or combine it with other information about you.

### Who receives it

GameAnalytics ApS processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. We do not share it with
anyone else. See
[GameAnalytics' privacy notice](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice) for how they
handle and keep data.

### How long it is kept

GameAnalytics keeps the data for as long as set out in its own privacy notice and data retention
policy, after which it is deleted or kept only in aggregated form that can't be tied to a device.

## Your choices

- **Say no, or change your mind:** use *Share play data* in the in-game Menu. When it is off, the
  game sends nothing more.
- **Delete everything on your device:** uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings.
  This also deletes the random ID, so any future data can't be linked to past data.
- **Ask about or delete data already sent:** email us at the contact address above. Because the data
  is anonymous and not linked to you, we may not be able to find which data is yours. You can also
  contact GameAnalytics at privacy@gameanalytics.com.

If you live in the EU, the UK or another region with similar laws, you have rights to access,
correct, delete and object to the processing of personal data. Contact us to use them. We rely on
your consent for analytics, and you can withdraw it at any time in the Menu.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If
you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared play data, contact us and we will
help delete it.

## Permissions

The Android app requests only:

- **Internet** (`INTERNET`): to send play data, if you agree.
- **Network state** (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): to check for a connection before sending, so data
  waits until you are online.

It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or advertising ID permissions.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change affects what
data is collected, the game will ask for your agreement again.

oddlycodes · support@oddlycodes.com

# Google Play: Data safety answers

These are the answers given in the Play Console *Data safety* form, based on what this version of
the game sends.

**Data collection and security**

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | Yes |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Yes |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | Yes, by email (see *Your choices*) |

**Data types**

All of these are **collected, not shared**, **optional** (only sent if the player agrees), processed
**not ephemerally**, and used for **Analytics** only.

| Category | Data type | Collected | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Approximate location | Yes | Country, worked out by GameAnalytics from the IP address |
| App activity | App interactions | Yes | Gameplay events: objectives, clues, answers, conversations |
| App info and performance | Crash logs | Yes | Error and crash reports |
| App info and performance | Diagnostics | Yes | Session length, device model, OS version, game version |
| Device or other IDs | Device or other IDs | Yes | The random ID created by the game (not the advertising ID) |

Not collected: personal info (name, email, user IDs, address, phone), financial info, health and
fitness, messages, photos and videos, audio, files and docs, calendar, contacts, precise location,
web browsing, installed apps, other user-generated content.

"Not shared": GameAnalytics is a service provider processing data on the developer's behalf, which
Google Play does not count as sharing.

# Ads

**This game contains no ads.**

- No ad networks or ad SDKs are included.
- The game does not use the Android advertising ID. The app does not request the
  `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission, and GameAnalytics is set to use a random ID
  instead.
- Play Console answers: *Contains ads:* **No**. *Does your app use advertising ID?* **No**.
